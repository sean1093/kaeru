## Method and honesty note

The system starts 2026-11-01. Nobody has yet been through a full airport kiosk flow under it. Evidence therefore comes from three places, and the table says which:

1. **Official warnings** — the things Japan Tourism Agency / National Tax Agency explicitly tell travelers not to do. A rule that needs an official warning is a pain point by definition.
2. **Early real experience** — Taiwanese travelers who already met refund-method-style flows in 2026, because some shops (animate with Ocean, mont-bell and others with PIE VAT) ran them ahead of the deadline. This is the closest thing to first-hand data that exists, and it is where the fee problem surfaced.
3. **Anticipatory worry** — what Taiwanese and English-language travelers are asking and fearing right now.

Severity = damage if it happens. Frequency = how many travelers meet it. Both on a 1–5 scale; **Rank** is the product, tie-broken by how cheaply Kaeru can fix it.

## What this implies for the product

Five conclusions, in priority order:

1. **The airport gate is the product.** PP-01, PP-02, PP-09 and PP-19 are all one moment, they are all irreversible, and they are all solvable by an offline, per-receipt, per-passport checklist that refuses to let the user check bags early. Everything else is supporting cast.
2. **Be honest about money.** PP-03 and PP-07 mean a headline "¥12,400 coming back" is a lie by omission. Show tax paid, estimated net after known fees, and actually-received. Users who have been burned once will not forgive an optimistic number.
3. **Default to "I don't know yet".** PP-04 and PP-05: the traveler genuinely does not know the operator at the till. Any design that demands it at logging time will be abandoned in the shop doorway.
4. **Teach two counter-intuitive rules, repeatedly.** Receipt-unit rejection (PP-02) and "unpackaged still means unused" (PP-08). Both are places where the system behaves opposite to intuition.
5. **Rules are data.** PP-18 plus the scheduled 2027-04-01 food rate cut to 1% mean the correct rate and the correct system depend on the purchase date. Hard-coding either is a dated bug.
